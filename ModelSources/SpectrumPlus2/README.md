# Sinclair ZX Spectrum +2 — original grey

The **1986 grey +2**, not a black +2A/+2B or floppy-drive +3.
The original geometry includes the sloping split shell, recessed 58-key keyboard,
Datacorder lid/window and six buttons, twin left joystick sockets/reset,
rear heatsink opening and characteristic original-128K-derived port arrangement.
The two packed textures are newly typeset artwork, not photographs.

Collector-reported nominal envelope: 439 × 174 × 58 mm including 2 mm feet.
The exported exterior is approximately **438.561 × 174.080 × 57.907 mm**;
bevels and thin artwork surfaces account for the small difference.
No serial number or exact PCB revision is asserted.

Rear/side legends use orthonormal mapping at 32 pixels/mm, preserving text
aspect rather than stretching fixed-width labels. `case-label-layout.json`,
`artwork-layout.json` and both prepared PNG atlases are required build inputs.
Profiles, radii, lower-case inset, seam, socket dimensions, contact details and
cassette mechanisms remain inferred. Primary keys are represented; some small
secondary legends are incomplete. Hidden underside/serial details are omitted.
