# Timex Sinclair 2068 — US / NTSC

The approved model represents the **US-market silver Timex Sinclair 2068**,
not the Portuguese Timex Computer 2068. It retains US Sinclair branding,
TS2068 keyboard legends and the narrow original cartridge-bay form.
Portuguese branding, PAL markings, taller TC cartridge opening and TC expansion
wiring are not incorporated. No serial number or exact PCB issue is asserted.

The original geometry includes a split sloping case, 42 hard keycaps with broad
flat tops and small perimeter bevels, blind key/ventilation wells, a recessed
closed cartridge lid, two nine-pin joystick sockets, a rocker switch, five rear
round sockets and an expansion cavity. The 117 narrow ventilation openings
approximate the photographic rhythm; their exact historic count/pitch is not certified.

Published envelope: **374.058 × 190.600 × 42.000 mm**, including the rocker
and rear connector lips. The scale anchor is a collector-reported 373 × 190 ×
39 mm case with 3 mm feet. Key height is relative to the local sloping deck,
not an additional 3.5 mm above the highest rear case point.

Case profiles, radii, seam heights, key dimensions, lid/well depth and absolute
port placement are photo-inferred approximations. The underside was not supplied:
feet, screw wells and internal construction are conservative generic estimates.
Connector contacts are illustrative, not an electrical pinout or fit specification.
The 374 closed meshes form a presentation assembly, not one printable solid.

Four necessary textures are packed in both model files. They contain rectified
US photographic artwork regions, redrawn primary key glyphs, isolated TCC lid
markings and newly drawn rear labels. Crop aspect is preserved; no whole source
photograph is stretched across a case side. Some photographed cap shading,
softness and perspective residuals remain. Colors/roughness and typography are
not calibrated production references.

Open `timexSinclair2068.blend` to edit, or `timexSinclair2068.usdz` to view.
No original input photograph, private comparison, raw report, backup,
standalone texture folder or generation pipeline is included.
