# Acorn Electron

`electron-review` is the retained filename of the visually reviewed model.
It contains a rounded split-shell loft, recessed keyboard, 56 tapered and
dished keycaps, rear vent banks, connector openings, badge grid and newly
drawn legends. No photographic pixels are used as textures.

Nominal envelope: **340 × 160 × 65 mm**, supported by Retro Isle and a Micromundo
distributor brochure. Treating 65 mm as the total height including feet/keys
is a modeling assumption: the sources do not specify that measurement convention.
The builder normalizes its photo-shaped Z profile to this envelope, then
constructs circular sockets so the bores are not stretched into ellipses.

Local slopes, radii, key pitch/sculpting, special-key staggering and socket
locations remain photo-based estimates. Port contacts are illustrative, not
electrical specifications. The underside is plain with provisional feet; hidden
vents, screw patterns and labels are not invented. The unverified power-entry
side is left plain. Branding and BASIC legends are approximate original artwork.

`keyboard-layout.json` and three `textures/*.png` images are the builder's
required external inputs. No photograph, brochure or raw-reference folder is needed.
