# Retro84 Touch — original fictional membrane variant

Retro84 Touch is the budget, membrane-keyboard member of the original fictional
Retro84 family. It is **printed pressure-sensitive polyester, not a modern glass
touchscreen, capacitive display or LCD**. Sixty-four flat printed zones retain
the original layout, including four amber function zones; the badge reads
“RETRO | 84 / TOUCH KEYBOARD.”

The case, seam, feet, ports, main PCB and family badge shape retain the original
**364 × 218.747 × 50.25 mm** envelope. Seventy-five family meshes retain their
geometry, UVs and transforms; the badge uses the Touch print material.
The original cap/switch/stabilizer/keyboard-plate assemblies are replaced,
not hidden under the printed surface. The finished Touch edition contains
**113 closed meshes and 33,670 triangles**.

Thin printed films sit 0.15 mm below the case deck. A spacer with 64 contact
windows separates illustrative conductive targets; insulating laminate, a
carrier and small clips support the membrane. There are no raised keycaps or
tactile rims. The design suggests inexpensive, nearly travel-free typing, but
does not provide a cost-certified bill of materials, working scan electronics,
flex routing, tooling tolerances or an interactive keyboard.

## Editing and game use

Open `retro84Touch.blend` to edit the geometry, materials and five packed artwork
images, or `retro84Touch.usdz` to view. `Touch_*` parts sit under
`Assembly_Keyboard`. The original [Retro84](../Retro84/) remains a separate,
unchanged model; choosing Touch does not modify its assets.

For the game's Keyboard Computer representation, **“Membrane / Chiclet” selects
Touch**; rubber, full-travel and mechanical choices retain the original concept
illustration. This is an image/model selection, not dynamic construction of
every component or case style. Other case styles keep their own illustrations.
Pricing and component-selection rules are unchanged; no fixed price, CPU, RAM
or electrical rating is printed into this design.

Family plastics retain `Case_upper` / `Case_lower` and separate rubber/metal
materials. Membrane print backgrounds and the rear-label background are baked
into packed atlases; recoloring can require matching artwork edits, not only
plastic-material changes. Preserve UV scale and repack edited images.

Only finished model files, the transparent model-only preview and usage/credits
are distributed. No original inputs, comparison sheets, backups or construction
pipeline is included.
