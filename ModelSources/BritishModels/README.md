# BBC Micro Model B and Amstrad CPC 464

## BBC Micro

The 411 × 350 × 73 mm exterior was selected within supplied dimensional ranges.
A supplied miniature informed case planes and keyboard relationships, not
full-size hardware metrology. Neither the miniature mesh nor separate measurement
inputs are distributed; the finished scene contains newly constructed geometry.

The sloping cream two-piece case has a continuous recessed keyboard/legend
strip, 74 tapered/dished keys, ten red function keys and three indicators.
Cursor arrows and COPY use darker gray keys. RETURN lettering has an
aspect-correct wide atlas region. The upper rear cover meets the lower case
flush. Rear photograph and owl-mask pixels are retained in prepared textures.
Small legends, underside features and connector interiors remain simplified.

## Amstrad CPC 464

Nominal 580 × 170 × 70 mm envelope includes raised keys and feet.
The sloping case follows supplied top/side photographs, with 74 raised keys,
stepped Enter, a spacebar/cursor-shaped keyboard opening, cassette door and
six shallow transport buttons. Individual caps reuse registered photographic
artwork. Tape spools, fine printing and most rear/side connectors remain
photographic details. Regional markings may differ between the supplied views.
The shell's front/rear height split is photo-fitted, not separately measured.

Both are closed-part visual assemblies, not printable manufacturing solids.
Edit their geometry/materials directly in Blender; necessary artwork is packed.
Original supplied images and construction inputs are not included separately.
