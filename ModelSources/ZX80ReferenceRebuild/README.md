# Sinclair ZX80

The exterior includes the full lower case, blue 40-key membrane
keyboard, supplied Sinclair/ZX80 top decal, seven cosmetic rear stripes and
seven **case-colored plastic joining rivets**. The underside retains the
reference-informed grooves, troughs, recesses and asymmetric rear clearances;
printing supports and hidden reference-CAD internals are not included.

The rear has **three case apertures**: a shared power/EAR/MIC opening,
a circular video/RF opening and the expansion edge-connector opening.
The modeled PCB is **1.6 mm thick**, with **23 thin gold fingers on each face**.
Its lower support is relieved so it does not intersect the board/contacts or
obscure access to the lower fingers. Contacts are thin surface representations,
not raised bars. Gold plating thickness, jack interiors and electrical pinout
are illustrative rather than manufacturing specifications.

Envelope: approximately **174.600 × 218.343 × 40.883 mm**. Unitless supplied
case references were interpreted as millimetres, corroborated by CAD metadata.
That is not independent physical metrology. Profiles, small radii, rivet heads,
socket depths and hidden features remain reference-based approximations.
The 73 closed meshes form an exterior assembly, not one printable solid.

Both necessary artwork images are packed in the Blender/USDZ files.
The supplied top decal retains its full aspect ratio and ink layout, with the
white matte removed and composited onto the case. Its modeled size is
76 × 28.656 mm; exact historical print size and colors are not calibrated.
Keyboard lettering and secondary block/checker glyphs are newly drawn;
typeface and raster-edge quality limit extreme closeups.

Open `zx80.blend` to edit or `zx80.usdz` to view. No original supplied decal,
photograph, STL/CAD file, research report or construction pipeline is included
separately.
